## Design Context

### Users

Small and mid-sized SaaS, technical service, and digital teams use this workspace to search trusted product and internal knowledge, ask an AI assistant questions, and manage workspace access. Administrators also maintain knowledge bases, files, invitations, members, and permissions.

### Brand Personality

Trustworthy, calm, precise, and efficient. The interface should make bilingual teams feel confident that knowledge is traceable and access is controlled.

### Aesthetic Direction

Refined minimalism for a focused AI workbench: content-first layouts, restrained surfaces, clear hierarchy, and equal-quality light and dark themes. Preserve the existing quiet, professional visual language and treat bilingual support as a first-class interaction rather than an add-on.

### Design Principles

- Keep the workspace calm and content-first; avoid decorative UI that competes with answers or settings.
- Maintain parity between Chinese and English: every user-facing label, status, action, tooltip, and error should have a translation.
- Make permissions and destructive actions explicit, scannable, and easy to recover from.
- Preserve responsive behavior and accessible labels while allowing translated text to breathe.
- Prefer small, reversible interactions such as the user-menu language switch and persisted local preference.
